# Biz Helper SA

An AI tool for small South African businesses (spaza shops, salons, street food, township traders).

The owner types their business name, what they sell and the prices. The tool writes:

- a ready-to-share price list
- a Facebook post
- a WhatsApp broadcast message

in English, isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, Afrikaans or an English + isiZulu mix.

## Status

First version: a single-page website (`index.html`). The AI part currently runs inside Claude.
